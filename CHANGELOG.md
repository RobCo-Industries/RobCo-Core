# Changelog

## Unreleased

- The `audio_rest_mid()` ramp never moves the duty by more than its largest step between two writes. Before, an IRQ late past the ramp's end let the pin jump from a low duty straight to 128. Now a late IRQ skips samples only while the step stays that small, and otherwise the ramp takes longer. It always writes its last sample, so `op=pcm_end` reads `last=881`. The `op=pcm` line and the ramp bytes are unchanged.
- `audio_pcm_mix()` returns `AUDIO_ERR_STATE` when a one-shot play's last sample has sounded and its end IRQ is still due. Before, it returned `0` and printed `op=pcm_mix` for an overlay that never sounded. New engine call `audio_pcm_join_plays()`.
- `audio_rest_mid()`: the call plays a 40 ms ramp from duty 0 to duty 128. Once the ramp reaches its end, every silence the PWM backend writes is duty 128 at the PCM range. A sample that starts near the midpoint then makes no jump. A stop, a cut or a priority start before the ramp ends cancels it: the rest level stays duty 0 and a later call can retry. A kernel that never calls it keeps today's pin levels and trace. The idle pin then carries the 37.5 kHz carrier all the time. None of this ran on a board.
- `audio_pcm_mix()` and `audio_pcm_mix_busy()`: one overlay buffer added to the PCM that plays, clamped, before the volume. It also takes a play that waits for its tick. The trace prints `op=pcm_mix len crc t_ms` at its start, with a 64-bit time, so a loop past 71 minutes keeps it right. The overlay and a late queued buffer start at the play's next grid sample that has not passed, so a late sample IRQ skips none of their bytes. Both are placed by a sample index on the play's one grid, not by a time, so at 22050 Hz no overlay byte repeats or is skipped, and late queues in a row do not drift. `audio_pcm_mix()` and `audio_pcm_queue()` refuse the ramp with `AUDIO_ERR_STATE`, and `audio_rest_mid()` returns `AUDIO_ERR_BUSY` while a stop waits for its tick. New engine calls `audio_pcm_join()`, `audio_pcm_join_us()` and `audio_pcm_chain_at()`, and the `last_n` field. `audio_pcm_chain_at()` compares the end in samples on 64 bits: a queue behind a play that ends more than 2^31 us away no longer switches at once or loops in the IRQ-masked call. The engine computes sample times on 64 bits, with at most two correction turns. Not heard on a board yet.
- The audio guide allows `audio_play_pcm()` and `audio_pcm_mix()` in a tick callback for short buffers, and says why the buffer must be short.
- `examples/audio` gains the `pcm_mix` and `rest` cases, and `tests/target/test_audio.py` checks them.
- `audio_pcm_scale()` rounds to the nearest step, a half step away from 128. Before, it cut toward 128, which flattened a quiet sine at any volume under 100. Every sample play at a volume under 100 now puts slightly different duty values on the pin. `audio_pcm_scale(255, 50)` is now `192`, was `191`.
- `robco-ci.yml` marks the checkout and every submodule folder, at any depth, as safe for git in both jobs, then checks that git works in each. Before, git refused the checkout in the container with "dubious ownership".
- The harness plugin removes a passing test's `tmp_path` when the test ends. A test that fails or errors in setup, call or teardown keeps its folder. Set `ROBCO_KEEP_TMP=1` to keep every folder. A project that sets `tmp_path_retention_policy` itself keeps its own value.
- `rimg_seal()` and `rimg_pack()` in `src/lib/rimg_seal.c`: the sealer and the packer in C. A host test runs every vector of `tests/vectors/rimg/vectors.ini` through them.
- `tools/rimg.py` reads a PBM icon width or height as 1 to 9 ASCII digits, as the spec asks. It now refuses `+32`, `-32`, `3_2`, `0000000032` and a token with a vertical tab or a form feed with `io`, as the C sealer does. Seven new vectors check it.
- `.gitattributes` turns off end-of-line conversion for test vectors, golden images and byte-compared fixtures, so a Windows checkout with `core.autocrlf=true` keeps their bytes. `tests/tools/test_vector_eol.py` checks it for `tests/vectors/`.
